About

About me.

I came from Agronomy and ended up in code — it's in this meeting between the field and technology that I build best.

I'm a solution engineer passionate about connecting technical knowledge with technology to solve real problems. I enjoy closing the complete loop: front-end, back-end, database, infrastructure and the user experience.

Today I work at the intersection of agronomy, software development and data analysis — building rural management systems, process automation, dashboards and applications with geotechnologies and artificial intelligence. From soil fertility to APIs, from interactive maps to automated workflows.

What drives me is building systems that help people work better. In my free time, I turn ideas into personal projects — SaaS, automations, prototypes and proofs of concept you can open and try.
